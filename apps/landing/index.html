Catch freight document errors before customs does.

DocCheck reads every paper of a shipment — invoice, packing list, bill of lading, ISF, air waybill, certificates — cross-checks them against each other and against the destination's customs rules, and links each finding to the exact line in the PDF and the regulation behind it. Then it helps you get corrected documents from the supplier.

How it works

A model reads. Plain code checks.

A local model only copies the values as printed. Tested code normalizes them and deterministic rules decide, with the tolerances your organization sets, so every finding can be explained and replayed. The model comes back only for the narrow cases a rule cannot settle alone.

Drop in the documents

PDFs, scans, phone photos or spreadsheets, by upload, email or a watched folder. A combined PDF is split into its documents and each one is classified.

53 rules cross-check them

Counts and weights across documents, invoice arithmetic, Incoterms, container check digits, ISF 10+2, HS codes, denied parties and the fields each customs authority requires.

Fix it before arrival

Each finding opens the PDF on the cited line. Ask the supplier for corrections from the app; their reply and corrected PDF come back into the shipment.

Reading documents

  • Any format: text PDFs, scans (OCR), phone photos straightened and cropped, .xlsx and .csv read without a model.
  • Combined PDFs split by page into their documents, each one classified.
  • Review queue: unsure values beside the PDF, checked from the keyboard.
  • Supplier learning: your corrections become examples for that supplier's next documents.

Rules and compliance

  • 53 rules across documents, each one tunable per organization: on or off, severity, tolerances, carriers.
  • Tariff checks: HS codes against HTSUS and the Canadian Customs Tariff, partner agencies (FDA, USDA, CFIA…) and dual-use flags.
  • Party screening against OFAC, BIS, SEMA, UN, EU and UFLPA lists, with an allowlist and forced-labour risk flags.
  • Citations: every finding links to the CBSA, CBP, RGCE, EU or HMRC passage, found by hybrid search.

Suppliers and inbox

  • Correction requests drafted from the findings in three languages and sent over your SMTP.
  • An inbox for every conversation: unread, awaiting reply, overdue, not sent.
  • Supplier portal: a signed link to upload corrected PDFs, no account needed.
  • Pre-shipment checks: suppliers test draft invoices and packing lists before shipping.

Duties and landed cost

  • Customs value from the invoice and its Incoterm, under US and Canadian rules.
  • Dated rate tables: Section 301 / 232, Canadian surtaxes, MPF, HMF, GST and HST.
  • Free trade: CUSMA data elements, possible savings, AD/CVD and SIMA cases flagged.
  • Draft CBP 7501 or CBSA CAD, prefilled and never transmitted.

Analytics

  • KPIs: clean on first validation, findings per shipment, error rate by rule, supplier, origin and route.
  • Supplier scorecard with its formula on the page, and price benchmarks per product and HS-6.
  • Ask your data in plain language: SQL written locally, checked and run read-only.
  • Regulation watch: changed passages, the rules that cite them and the open shipments they affect.

Integrations

  • REST API with scoped, rate-limited keys and an OpenAPI 3.1 reference.
  • Signed webhooks, and Slack or Teams alerts.
  • Watched folders (SFTP, S3, local) and documents by email become shipments.
  • Exports: CargoWise XML, X12 856/310; container tracking checked against the BL.

Teams

  • Organizations and roles: owner, admin, analyst, reviewer, viewer and read-only clients.
  • Assignments, comments with @mentions, due dates and two-step approval of accepted risks.
  • Hash-chained audit log and records retention (CBSA 6 years, CBP 5).
  • Two-factor sign-in, single sign-on (OpenID Connect) and usage plans.

Extracted data

  • Summary of values, weights and origins across shipments.
  • Companies as one record however each document spells them.
  • Products with their price per purchase, seller and currency.
  • Containers with seal, carrier, vessel and route.

Models you control

  • Local by default: Ollama for extraction, the agent and embeddings; Tesseract or Unlimited-OCR for scans.
  • Optional providers: OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini, set by an admin with encrypted keys.
  • Measured: an evaluation page with field accuracy and per-rule recall and precision.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your infrastructure.

DocCheck runs on your own machine or server, with its own database. Nothing is sent out unless you choose to.

  • Your own Postgres database and API server, on hardware you control.
  • Local models through Ollama: no document goes to an outside AI service.
  • Rules and normalization are plain, tested code, never a model.
  • On a phone too: the web app installs as a PWA and turns camera photos into one straightened PDF.
  • Open source under the MIT license: read every line it runs.
